## tun2proxy 0.8.3

Project: `tun2proxy`

Source: https://github.com/tun2proxy/tun2proxy

Use in AMRI: Android userspace packet-forwarding layer from an already-created `VpnService` TUN file descriptor to an already-confirmed local SOCKS transport endpoint. AMRI does not use tun2proxy for route selection, credential storage or VPN protocol configuration.
